What it is

  • Link your sushi, break through. Steer a trailing line of sushi through the enemy horde. You pick the path; every plate attacks on its own.
  • One more plate, a new way to attack. Recruit sushi in the shop and merge three of the same sushi to level it up. Sushi Families add party synergies, and Specialties change how the whole platter plays.
  • Your prep makes every run different. Before service, Tetsuo offers Today's Prep: three options, each with a bonus and a cost.
  • Can that cat go even higher? After battle, Tetsuo and Yoshiko's grandkids run in with beckoning cats. White, gold, then rainbow: each upgrade raises the bonus.
  • Giant seafood incoming. An octopus, a tuna, a whale: Seafood Anomalies turn a familiar arena into a seafood frenzy.
  • To the final service, again and again. Twenty services with hordes, elites and a final boss. Win to unlock the next Challenge Level, up to 10.

Where it comes from

SUSHISNK started with a family sushi restaurant. The developer's grandfather ran a sushi shop in Japan for many years; in 2026 its long history came to an end and the building was set to be torn down. Wanting to keep something of it, the developer began making a sushi game. The beckoning cats that show up all over the game come from the many maneki-neko figurines that sat in that shop.

About AI use

Generative AI tools are used in certain parts of development: programming assistance, translation of in-game text, and some visual assets including sushi character artwork. Generated images are selected, refined and integrated by the developer, and all decisions on concept, mechanics and design are the developer’s. Nothing is generated in real time during play. (Same as the AI disclosure on the Steam store page.)
